Google Forms Choice Limiter for Slots and Quotas

Choice limits are useful when a Google Form option represents a limited slot: appointment times, workshop sessions, meal choices, volunteer roles, inventory items, or interview windows.

What FormGuard can do

FormGuard can watch supported choice questions and replace an option when its quota is filled. The replacement text is (All options filled), which tells later visitors that the option is no longer available.

Good use cases

  • Appointment slots where each time can only be selected once or a few times.
  • Event sessions with limited seats.
  • Volunteer assignments with a fixed number of openings.
  • Simple inventory choices where each choice maps to a quota.

Set up choice limits

  1. Add your multiple-choice, checkbox, or dropdown options in Google Forms.
  2. Open FormGuard from the add-ons menu.
  3. Turn on Choice eliminator.
  4. Set the quota for each supported question.
  5. Save settings and submit a test response.
For quantity-based sales, such as buying three plants in one response, a simple choice quota may not be enough. Test the workflow on a copy of your form before using it for live inventory.

Use it with response limits

Choice limits can run alongside a total response limit. That means one form can close at a total capacity while also removing individual options as they fill.
