Schedule Google Forms to Open and Close Automatically

Some Google Forms should only collect responses during a specific window: applications, event signups, daily intake forms, office-hour requests, or limited-time surveys.

Use scheduled form controls when

  • A request window starts at a known time.
  • A form should close after a deadline.
  • You want to reopen a form after reviewing or resetting the workflow.
  • The same form link or QR code should stay available for later windows.

Set a close or reopen time

  1. Open your Google Form.
  2. Open FormGuard from the add-ons menu.
  3. Turn on Timer.
  4. Choose a close time, a reopen time, or both.
  5. Save settings.
Google Apps Script time triggers are not second-level schedulers. Timer actions usually run within a few minutes of the scheduled time, depending on Google's trigger scheduling.

Combine timers with limits

Scheduled open and close windows can run with a response limit. The form closes when the earliest configured condition is met, such as reaching capacity or passing the deadline.

Best fit

Use this for operational control, not for high-stakes second-level timing. For most classes, events, signups, and intake windows, a few minutes of scheduling variance is usually acceptable.
