Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install FormGuard?

Open a Google Form, go to Extensions → Add-ons → Get add-ons, search for "FormGuard", and click Install. Grant the required permissions and you're ready to go.

Why can't FormGuard access my other forms?

The updated scheduled-task build requests full Forms permission because a background job has no active editor and must reopen the form you configured by ID. It does not enumerate unrelated forms. If Google asks for new permission, complete the Google authorization flow and then save again.

My form didn't close at the exact time I set. Why?

The updated Pro timer uses Google Apps Script time-driven triggers, which can run later than the exact time selected. Test a copied form first and allow a buffer for critical deadlines.

Is billing enabled?

Free installation remains available. The updated personal Pro build is in staged testing and Google review preparation: $29 paid once for 12 months, without automatic renewal. Confirm access to the paid build with support before buying. See the activation guide.

Can I get a refund?

Creem handles payment and refund requests. A confirmed full refund revokes the paid license for that order; partial refunds are reviewed separately. Contact us with the order reference. Avoid sharing a full license key or card details in support messages.

How do I uninstall FormGuard?

Disable scheduled rules and save before uninstalling. Then use Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons to remove FormGuard. Uninstalling alone does not prove that every stored property or trigger was deleted; contact support if you need help checking older rules.

Is my data safe?

Form questions and responses are handled within Google services and are not sent to Creem or FormSuite's license service. The updated paid build sends an account email, signed license key, and product identifier over HTTPS for activation and access checks. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email us at lidaban2025@gmail.com with: (1) what you expected to happen, (2) what actually happened, and (3) steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots help!
